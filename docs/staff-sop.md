# Staff SOP — Using getinvoiceIQ

**SOP = Standard Operating Procedure.** These are the steps everyone on the team follows, so
every invoice is captured the same way and the food-cost numbers can be trusted.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Anyone who receives deliveries or handles supplier paperwork. Owner/manager for the weekly check. |
| **Where** | [getinvoiceiq.com](https://www.getinvoiceiq.com), on a phone or computer |
| **Golden rule** | **Every supplier invoice goes into getinvoiceIQ the same day it arrives.** |

---

## 1. When a delivery arrives (every delivery, about 2 minutes)

1. **Check the delivery against the invoice before signing.** Count cases, check weights, look
   for damaged or missing items. Write any shortage on the paper invoice and get the driver to
   initial it.
2. **Capture the invoice.** Use whichever way is easiest:

   | Way | How |
   |---|---|
   | **Camera scan** (best for paper) | Open getinvoiceIQ → **Add invoice** (pink **+**) → **Scan with camera**. Lay the invoice flat on a dark surface in good light. Hold steady; it takes the shot when the whole page is sharp, or tap to snap. |
   | **Upload** | **Add invoice** → choose the PDF or photo from your phone. |
   | **Email** | If the supplier emails invoices, nothing to do. getinvoiceIQ scans the connected inbox every day. |
   | **WhatsApp** | Send the photo or PDF to the restaurant's getinvoiceIQ WhatsApp number. |

3. **Multi-page invoices:** capture every page. A missing page means missing lines and a wrong total.
4. **Keep the paper copy** in the invoice folder until the end of the month.

> **Don't** take photos at an angle, with fingers over the numbers, or with two invoices in one photo.

## 2. Review queue (daily, usually under 5 minutes)

Some invoices wait in **Review** before they count:
- **Every email and WhatsApp invoice**, because anyone could send one.
- **Any invoice whose math doesn't add up**: lines don't match the subtotal, quantity × price
  doesn't match the line total, or the vendor or date couldn't be read.

For each invoice in **Review**:

1. Open it next to the original (paper, PDF or photo).
2. Check: **vendor name, invoice date, invoice total**, and that the number of lines matches.
3. **Vendor name wrong?** Tap it, correct it, save.
4. **Everything matches?** Tap **Approve**.
5. **Lines or total read wrong?** Tap **Deny**, then capture the invoice again with a clearer,
   flatter photo (or the PDF). Line items can't be edited in the app yet.
6. **Not a supplier invoice** (personal bill, junk email, another restaurant's invoice)? Tap
   **Deny**. It is removed from the books.

> An invoice in Review is **not** counted in spend or food cost until it's approved. A long Review
> queue makes every number on the dashboard too low.

## 3. Price alerts (when the bell shows a number)

1. Tap the **bell** (or **Alerts** in the menu).
2. For each price increase: is it real, or a misread (for example a case price read as a single
   unit)? If it's a case or jug read as one unit, set **1 on invoice =** for that ingredient on
   the Recipes page. If the invoice itself was misread, tell the owner. It needs a re-capture.
3. Real increases: tell the owner/manager, especially on high-volume items (proteins, oil,
   dairy).
4. Dismiss the alert once it has been handled.

## 4. Weekly check (owner or manager, every Monday, about 15 minutes)

1. **Review queue is empty.** Approve or deny anything left.
2. **Home → Weekly:** total spend vs last week. A big jump? Find out which invoice.
3. **Food cost %:** the 30-day number and the weekly trend. Most full-service restaurants aim for
   **28–35%**. Above that, look at price alerts and the usage table.
4. **Recipes → Theoretical vs actual usage:** the biggest positive **$ impact** rows are where
   money leaks (over-portioning, waste, theft, or a wrong recipe). Pick the top one and check it
   in the kitchen this week.
5. **Pack sizes** (menu → Pack sizes): tick **Needs pack size** and fill in what one case of
   each product holds, for example cilantro 20 × 200 g, yogurt 4 × 1 lb, Costco cornstarch
   30 × 12 oz. Sizes printed in the product name are filled in for you; check and **Save**.
   Every purchase then shows in pounds, so stores compare per pound.

## 5. Recipes (owner or chef, once, then when the menu changes)

1. **Recipes** → for your top-selling dishes, tap **Draft with AI**.
2. Check each ingredient and amount against how the kitchen actually makes it. Amounts are per
   plate, **after** trimming (peeled onion, trimmed chicken).
3. Tap **Save & confirm recipe**. The plate cost, food cost % and margin then update with every new invoice.
4. Prep items made in batches (N65 sauce, pre-fried chicken, gravies, ginger-garlic paste, dough)
   go under **Components**. Enter the batch recipe and what one batch yields after cooking (10 lb raw
   chicken → 7 lb fried). Then pick them in a dish from **Your prep items** and enter how many oz
   the dish uses. A prep item can use another prep item.
5. Ingredients bought in bottles or bags can be entered as **pack**: "2 pack" of yogurt = 2 bottles,
   priced by the pound from the pack size.
6. Set **Usable %** for ingredients with trim loss (whole chicken ≈ 70%, onion ≈ 90%).

> Start with the 10 dishes that sell the most. The usage report only becomes useful once those
> have recipes.

## 6. Month end (owner)

1. Review queue is empty, and every paper invoice in the folder is in getinvoiceIQ.
2. Check **Invoices** totals per vendor against supplier statements.
3. Note the month's **Food cost %** for your records.
4. File or shred the paper invoices according to your accounting rules.

---

## Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Invoice not showing up | Check **Review**: it may be waiting for approval. |
| Total looks wrong | Compare the invoice with the paper. If it was misread, the owner denies it and it's captured again with a clearer photo. |
| A dish shows a "+" plate cost | One ingredient has no price yet. See **Ingredients with no price yet** on the Recipes page. |
| A price looks far too high | It's probably a case or jug read as one unit. Set **1 on invoice =** for that ingredient. |
| Food cost % looks impossible (over 100%) | Sales may be missing. Check that Clover is connected and synced in **Settings**. |

*Questions or something not working? Use **Ask** in the app, or contact your getinvoiceIQ admin.*
